Research & Publications

2023

Comparative Analysis of the Fitness of Gamma, Log-normal, and Weibull Distributions to Japanese Income Data

Xinya Li, Akio Suzugawa

Master's Graduation Thesis · Hokkaido University

While Gamma, log-normal, and Weibull distributions share similar PDF shapes, comparative fitting analysis on economic data remains scarce. This thesis assesses fitting performance using Japanese household income data via maximum likelihood estimation and goodness-of-fit testing, providing guidance for distribution selection in applied econometrics.

Statistical ModellingIncome DistributionJapan
2022

American Sign Language Alphabet Recognition Using Inertial Motion Capture System with Deep Learning

Yutong Gu, Sherrine, Weiyi Wei, Xinya Li, Jianan Yuan, Masahiro Todoh

Inventions, 2022, 7(4):112

Proposes an ASL alphabet recognition system using inertial measurement unit (IMU) motion capture gloves combined with deep learning classifiers. The system achieves robust real-time recognition of hand gestures from sensor data, contributing to accessible human-computer interaction for the deaf and hard-of-hearing community.

Xinya Li, Yao Liu

Storage, Transportation and Preservation of Commodities, 2018(08):88–90

Examines the drop-and-pull transport mode — where tractor units decouple from semi-trailers to improve fleet utilization — and its standardization in China. Analyses operational models, efficiency gains, and regulatory frameworks supporting broader adoption across domestic logistics networks.
